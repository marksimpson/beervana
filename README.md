# Beervana 2026

Every beer at every stand, on your phone, ranked by what Untappd drinkers make of
it. Works with no signal, which matters once the stadium fills up.

## https://marksimpson.github.io/beervana/

Open that, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It launches full screen with its
own icon. Do it before you go and the app will work inside the venue whether or
not you have reception.

## The tabs

**Stands** — all 45 stands in the order you walk past them, starting at the
entrance by aisle 16 and wrapping round the concourse. Tap one to see what it is
pouring.

**Top rated** — every beer at the festival, best rated first.

**All beers** — the full list of 379.

**Leaderboard** — the festival's own live praise count, refreshed while you use
the app. Good for spotting what everyone else has found.

The search box filters everything by beer, brewery or style. Type "sour" or
"stout" to browse a style.

## Reading a beer

The green number is its Untappd rating. Some beers show a word instead:

- **New** — it is on Untappd but barely anyone has rated it. Festival one-offs,
  mostly. You would be among the first.
- **N/A** — could not be found on Untappd at all.

Tap any beer for its ABV, the brewery's own tasting notes, and a button that
opens it in the Untappd app so you can check in there. Beers that could not be
matched offer a search instead.

## Keeping track

**Mark as had it** ticks a beer off. It greys out, drops down the lists, and the
stand counters update so you can see what is left at each one.

That is stored on your phone alone. It does not check you in to Untappd, and
checking in on Untappd does not tick it here — they are separate.

## The two header buttons

**⇅** changes the order of whichever list you are on: walking direction or A to Z
on Stands, and rating, beer name or brewery on All beers.

**⚙** is settings, which includes a reset that clears everything the app has
stored on your phone.

## Worth knowing

The beer list is fixed, but the Untappd ratings and the leaderboard refresh
themselves whenever you open the app with a signal. Ratings move a lot during a
session, so a beer showing New at lunchtime may have a real score by evening.

Of the 379 beers, 237 have an established Untappd rating, 62 are too new to have
one, and 80 could not be matched — mostly the cocktails, ciders and one-offs.
